# fixtures

Copied from https://github.com/wxylib/fixtures at commit
e203c85f322bae598ec257763ae6f060937f3cc7, for the appendix figures of this post:

- `cf/oisst-like.zarr`: an OISST-style Zarr v3 store as xarray writes it
  (packed int16, latitude ascending, longitude 0 to 360)
- `curvilinear/swath-120x160.tif` and `swath-120x160-lonlat.tif`: a
  120 x 160 curved swath and its two-band longitude/latitude array, centre
  coordinates

See that repository for the scripts that make them.
